Refund Policy

Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected. The money is credited to the original payment method. If a refund has not arrived after 10 business days, please contact support with your order ID.

Password Reset

To reset your password, click Forgot Password on the login page and enter your registered email. You will receive a reset link that is valid for 30 minutes. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder.

Order Tracking

You can track your order from My Orders using your order ID. Orders usually ship within 2 business days and are delivered within 3 to 7 business days. If an order shows delivered but you did not receive it, raise a ticket within 48 hours.

Payment Failed Troubleshooting

If your payment fails but money is deducted, the amount is automatically reversed by the bank within 5 to 7 business days. If the amount is deducted twice, please raise a ticket with your transaction ID so our billing team can verify it.

Application Login Problems

If you cannot log in, make sure caps lock is off and try resetting your password. After 5 failed attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes. Clear your browser cache and try again if the page stays blank.
